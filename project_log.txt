Task 1 Completed – Player Shooting Efficiency

Analytic question:
Do forwards and midfielders differ significantly in their average shooting accuracy during the FIFA World Cup 2026?

Data source:
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Shooting Statistics

Data preparation:
- Retained players classified specifically as FW or MF
- Excluded players with zero shots
- Applied a minimum threshold of 3 shots
- Created Shooting_Accuracy from shots on target and total shots
- Checked missing values and duplicate records

Eligible population:
242 players
- 81 forwards
- 161 midfielders

Sampling:
Stratified random sample of 50 forwards and 50 midfielders

Key results:
- Forward mean shooting accuracy: 40.60%
- Midfielder mean shooting accuracy: 32.89%
- Forward 95% CI: 33.83%–47.36%
- Midfielder 95% CI: 26.29%–39.49%
- t-statistic: 1.639
- p-value: 0.104

Conclusion:
Forwards had a higher mean shooting accuracy in the sample, but the difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level.

## Analytic Task 3 – Team Possession and Interceptions

**Analytic question:**  
Do teams with below-median possession and teams with above-median possession differ significantly in their mean interceptions per 90 minutes at the FIFA World Cup 2026?

**Data sources:**  
FBref 2026 World Cup Squad Possession Statistics and Squad Miscellaneous Statistics.

**Data preparation:**  
- Joined the two datasets using `Squad`.
- Confirmed all 48 teams matched.
- Confirmed `90s` values agreed between both datasets.
- Created `Interceptions_per_90 = Int / 90s`.
- Median possession was 49.8%.
- Excluded two teams exactly equal to the median.
- No missing values were found.
- Randomly sampled 20 below-median and 20 above-median teams using `random_state=42`.

**Results:**  
- Above-median mean: 7.68 interceptions per 90.
- Below-median mean: 8.85 interceptions per 90.
- t-statistic = 1.906.
- p-value = 0.0649.
- At α = 0.05, the null hypothesis was not rejected.

**Conclusion:**  
Below-median possession teams had a higher sample mean, but there was insufficient statistical evidence to conclude that the population means differed significantly.
