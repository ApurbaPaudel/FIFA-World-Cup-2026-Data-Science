Task 1 Completed – Player Shooting Efficiency

Analytic question:
Do forwards and midfielders differ significantly in their average shooting accuracy during the FIFA World Cup 2026?

Data source:
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Shooting Statistics

Data preparation:
- Retained players classified specifically as FW or MF
- Excluded players with zero shots
- Applied a minimum threshold of 3 shots
- Created Shooting_Accuracy from shots on target and total shots
- Checked missing values and duplicate records

Eligible population:
242 players
- 81 forwards
- 161 midfielders

Sampling:
Stratified random sample of 50 forwards and 50 midfielders

Key results:
- Forward mean shooting accuracy: 40.60%
- Midfielder mean shooting accuracy: 32.89%
- Forward 95% CI: 33.83%–47.36%
- Midfielder 95% CI: 26.29%–39.49%
- t-statistic: 1.639
- p-value: 0.104

Conclusion:
Forwards had a higher mean shooting accuracy in the sample, but the difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level.
