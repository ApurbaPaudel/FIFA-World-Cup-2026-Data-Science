# Task 1 – Player Shooting Efficiency
**Question:**  
Do forwards and midfielders differ significantly in their average shooting accuracy during the FIFA World Cup 2026?

**Data source:**  
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Shooting Statistics

**Method:**  
Players were filtered to exact forward and midfielder positions and required to have at least three shots. Shooting accuracy was calculated as shots on target divided by total shots. A stratified random sample of 50 forwards and 50 midfielders was then analysed.

**Main result:**  
Forwards recorded a higher mean shooting accuracy of 40.60% compared with 32.89% for midfielders. However, the difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level (p = 0.104).
