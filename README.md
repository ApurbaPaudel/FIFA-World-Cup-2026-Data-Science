Objective 1: Four distinct analytic tasks using FIFA World Cup 2026 data.

## Analytic Task 1 – Player Shooting Efficiency

**Question:**  
Do forwards and midfielders differ significantly in their average shooting accuracy during the FIFA World Cup 2026?

**Data source:**  
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Shooting Statistics

**Status:** Completed

**Main result:**  
Forwards recorded a higher sample mean shooting accuracy (40.60%) than midfielders (32.89%). However, the two-sample t-test found that the difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level (p = 0.104).


## Analytic Task 3 – Team Possession and Interceptions

**Question:**  
Do teams with below-median possession and teams with above-median possession differ significantly in their mean interceptions per 90 minutes at the FIFA World Cup 2026?

**Data source:**  
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Squad Possession Statistics and Squad Miscellaneous Statistics

**Status:** Completed

**Main result:**  
Below-median possession teams recorded a higher sample mean of 8.85 interceptions per 90 minutes than above-median possession teams (7.68). However, the two-sample t-test found that the difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level (p = 0.0649).
