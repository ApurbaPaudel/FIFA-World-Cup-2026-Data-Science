## Analytic Task 1 – Player Shooting Efficiency

**Question:**  
Do forwards and midfielders differ significantly in their average shooting accuracy during the FIFA World Cup 2026?

**Data source:**  
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Shooting Statistics

**Status:** Completed

**Main result:**  
Forwards recorded a higher sample mean shooting accuracy (40.60%) than midfielders (32.89%). However, the two-sample t-test found that the difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level (p = 0.104).
