# Task 3 – Team Possession and Interceptions
**Question:**  
Do teams with below-median possession and teams with above-median possession differ significantly in their mean interceptions per 90 minutes at the FIFA World Cup 2026?

**Data sources:**  
FBref – FIFA World Cup 2026 Squad Possession Statistics and Squad Miscellaneous Statistics

**Method:**  
The two datasets were joined using `Squad`. Interceptions per 90 were calculated, teams were divided using the median possession value of 49.8%, and 20 teams were randomly sampled from each group.

**Main result:**  
Below-median possession teams recorded 8.85 interceptions per 90 on average, compared with 7.68 for above-median possession teams. The difference was not statistically significant at the 5% level (p = 0.0649).
